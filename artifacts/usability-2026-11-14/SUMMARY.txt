Candidate themes for the later evaluation
- All five encountered pattern double-click leading to Rename before discovering
  right-click > Edit notes. Some mistook the naming form's Keyboard for piano keys.
- Three encountered sampler/drum presets that left No sample/Empty states.
- The drum participant could not discover a clear mapping between pad sounds
  and piano-roll pitch rows.
- Several questioned the 0 plug-ins count despite visible built-in devices.
- Other observations cover major/minor color defaults, instrument/track identity,
  loop terminology, export naming, and dialog movement. Individual reports preserve
  exact steps, evidence, recovery, confidence, and successful discoveries.
